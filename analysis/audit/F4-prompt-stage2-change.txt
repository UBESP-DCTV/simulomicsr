- Group into the SAME replicate_group ONLY samples with IDENTICAL conditions

PROPOSTA — aggiungere una NUOVA sezione subito prima di "## Grouping guidelines",
e riscrivere il secondo bullet del grouping (quello su n_replicates):

  ## Input format: pre-deduplicated design conditions (IMPORTANT)
  Each entry in `samples:` is NOT an individual GSM but a DISTINCT experimental
  condition, already deduplicated across replicates upstream. For each entry:
  - `geo_accession` is a REPRESENTATIVE sample of the condition.
  - `n_replicates` is how many biologically-equivalent replicate samples the
    condition stands for.
  - `sample_facts` are the facts of the representative.
  Treat each entry as ONE condition that is ALREADY a replicate set of size
  `n_replicates`. Therefore:
  - Put the representative `geo_accession` (one per entry) into `sample_ids`; do
    NOT split an entry. Each entry is typically its own replicate_group.
  - Use `n_replicates` as the replicate count of that group (for quality /
    study_internal_score), even though `sample_ids` lists a single
    representative.
  - Merge two entries into the same group ONLY if they are truly the same
    condition.
  - RULE 4 ("do not omit") applies to every entry/condition.

  (e il bullet grouping su n_replicates diventa:)
  - Replicates are already merged upstream into one entry with `n_replicates`
    (see "Input format"). Do NOT re-split an entry into multiple groups, and do
    NOT merge distinct entries unless they are truly identical conditions.
